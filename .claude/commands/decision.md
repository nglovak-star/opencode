description: Work through a decision using the decision-support framework
argument-hint: <the decision you're facing>
---

Read `glovak-executive-assistant/core/instructions.md` first (reasoning
labels: FACT, SOURCE, INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION, ESTIMATE, UNKNOWN — never
present an assumption or inference as a fact).

Decision to analyze: $ARGUMENTS

1. If the decision isn't clear yet, ask exactly what's being decided —
   nothing else.
2. Using `glovak-executive-assistant/decisions/template.md`, work through:
   objective, options, advantages/disadvantages, costs, risks, opportunity
   cost, assumptions, unknowns, likely outcome, and a clear recommendation.
   Label every claim appropriately.
3. Append the analysis to
   `glovak-executive-assistant/decisions/decisions-log.md` and add a
   one-line entry to `glovak-executive-assistant/memory/decisions.md`.
4. Present the recommendation clearly, but the decision itself is Glovak's
   — don't act on it (e.g. don't send a communication or spend money) until
   he confirms, per the guardrails in `core/instructions.md`.
