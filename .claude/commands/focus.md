Read `glovak-executive-assistant/core/instructions.md` first.

1. Read `glovak-executive-assistant/tasks/tasks.md` and
   `glovak-executive-assistant/projects/projects.md`.
2. Identify the single highest-value next action right now — favor P1s,
   then P2s with near deadlines, then active project next-actions.
3. State it directly, in one or two sentences: what to do next and why it
   outranks everything else open.
4. If there's an obvious mismatch (e.g. Glovak just asked about something
   unrelated to the top priority), say so plainly, in the direct tone from
   `core/instructions.md` — this command exists specifically to cut through
   distraction.
